# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chemical engineering researchers and graduate students running Aspen Plus based extractive-distillation screening. They use the interface during long optimization runs, while writing reports, and while checking whether an agent-produced result is ready to be trusted.

## Product Purpose

The project connects molecular evidence, Aspen case generation, NSGA-II optimization, cost and energy analysis, and trust auditing for difficult binary separations such as C3F8/C3F6. The frontend should help users see progress, identify the current best tradeoff, inspect Pareto behavior, find stalled or boundary-hugging variables, and separate verified results from queued or blocked work.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, accountable. The UI should feel like a research control room: compact, legible, evidence-first, and careful about uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Do not make it look like a marketing SaaS dashboard, a decorative AI demo, or a glossy analytics landing page. Avoid nested cards, oversized hero sections, vague "AI agent" claims, gradient decoration, and colorful elements that do not encode state or meaning.

## Design Principles

1. Show evidence before claims.
2. Keep run state, data source, and freshness visible.
3. Make tradeoffs scannable without hiding raw values.
4. Separate verified, pending, and blocked states clearly.
5. Favor dense, stable layouts over expressive decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high contrast text, visible focus states, reduced-motion-safe transitions, responsive layouts for laptop and tablet screens, and color plus text or shape for status encoding.
